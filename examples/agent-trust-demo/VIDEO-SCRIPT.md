**ГОЛОС:**
> Our thesis is simple: manipulation is not a property of text alone.
> The same phrase — "there is no way out" — is pressure on a sales page,
> and a cry for help in a first-person message.
> pantheon-guard judges utterances in context. Never identities. Never people.
> It returns one of three decisions: pass, warn, or hold — and every verdict
> unfolds into the exact rule, idiom, or hypothesis that fired.

**ГОЛОС:**
> Now the team setting — this session is scripted, the verdict logic is real.
> Four agents work together. The sales agent gets a warning for cornering a customer — its trust score drops.
> An unsigned news item arrives, written like clickbait. We do not guess whether it is fake.
> We hold it at the provenance gate: no signature, pressure-style delivery — a human reviews it.
> Fake news generated by agents loses its cheapest path into the pipeline.
> A user in distress is routed to care, not to a verdict.
> And the research agent that honestly discloses uncertainty? Its reputation grows.
> Reputation applies to software agents only — scoring humans is a boundary we rejected by design.

## 3:20–4:05 · Как построено + честность

**ЭКРАН:** прокрутка к карте охвата («catches reliably / blind spots») и колонке «what this is not». Затем на секунду — строки `npm install pantheon-guard` и `inspect(text, { frame: 'sales' })`.

**ГОЛОС:**
> How it is built. Three complementary layers: deterministic lexical rules,
> a curated idiom registry, and a local zero-shot NLI model — mDeBERTa,
> about eighty milliseconds per hypothesis on a laptop CPU. No cloud calls. Sovereign by design.
> The whole product was built AI-first: I architect, AI tools write the code,
> and the language model acts as the teacher that distills understanding into
> a small, transparent, offline artifact — never as a runtime dependency.
> And here is our differentiator: we publish our blind spots next to our catches.
> Half-truths, fabricated authority — we say openly what we do not catch yet.
> A trust layer that hides its limits cannot be trusted itself.
